Before Computer Repair in Kampala: A Simple Checklist

Prepare for laptop or desktop support with a clear fault description, a backup plan and questions about diagnosis, costs and repair approval.

Describe the problem clearly

Write down the computer model, the exact error message and when the problem started. Note whether the fault appears while charging, connecting to Wi-Fi, opening a particular application or using an external device. A photograph of the error can help.

Discuss your files before work begins

Tell the technician whether important files are backed up. If the computer still works and it is safe to do so, copy essential files to storage you control. Agree what may be accessed, copied or erased before authorising repairs or a reinstall.

Bring the relevant accessories

If the problem concerns charging, bring the charger. For a printer or external device problem, explain the connected equipment. Contact the technician first to confirm what is needed and whether on-site assistance is appropriate.

Ask for diagnosis and a written estimate

  • What checks will be done, and is there a diagnostic fee?
  • Does the estimate separate labour, parts and other charges?
  • Will you be contacted before additional work or data erasure?
  • What is the expected turnaround time?
  • What warranty or follow-up support applies to the work?

Confirm the agreed scope and cost before work starts. A final diagnosis may require inspection, so an initial message cannot guarantee the repair or price.

Send a useful support request

“My [computer model] has [problem]. It started [when], and the error says [message]. I am in [location]. My important files are [backed up/not backed up]. Please advise on diagnosis, availability and the likely next steps.”
