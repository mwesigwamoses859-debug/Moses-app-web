Service Terms

These terms explain the practical expectations for booking and receiving MosesTechFix services.

Scope and pricing

We confirm the service scope, available options, and applicable service fee before work begins. Equipment, software licences, government fees, and third-party charges are separate unless we confirm otherwise in writing.

Device and account safety

Back up important files before a repair, setup, installation, or configuration service. Do not share passwords or verification codes unless they are necessary for a task you have requested.

Registration assistance

For URA TIN and passport services, MosesTechFix provides technical assistance, document checks, submission support, and appointment guidance. Official documents and final approvals are issued by the relevant government authority.

Questions before booking

Ask about your service, fee, timeline, or requirements before confirming. Contact MosesTechFix on WhatsApp for clarification.
