FAQ

Common questions before booking

Do you issue TINs or passports?

No. MosesTechFix assists with registration support, document checks, submission guidance, and appointment booking. Official documents are issued by the responsible government office.

What do I need for TIN or passport registration?

Start with your correct personal details, National ID or NIN details if available, business details for TIN support, and the passport type if you need passport help.

What are the application assistance fees?

URA registration assistance is UGX 100,000. Passport assistance is UGX 50,000. Government fees are separate where they apply.

Are course certificates included?

Yes, certificates of completion are included in the listed course packages, together with course materials and instructor support.

Can I learn online or in person?

Contact us with your preferred schedule and learning format. We will confirm the available option for your selected course.

Can you help me choose a computer?

Yes. Share your budget, intended use, and preferred device type, then we can recommend suitable laptop or desktop specifications.

Where can I send payment?

After confirming your service details, payments can be sent to 0789 218 570.
