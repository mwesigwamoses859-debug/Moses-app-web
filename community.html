Starting with real needs

Useful technology begins with a school’s plan.

  • We listen first. Each request begins with the school’s learners, equipment, and setting.

  • We assess carefully. Support is chosen for practical, safe, long-term use.

  • We report honestly. Updates are shared only after real work is complete.

A social-impact programme

A practical beginning, built around schools.

MosesTechFix Community is a social-impact programme of MosesTechFix Solutions. It is not a registered NGO. We are beginning with useful, school-focused support in Uganda.

Community support stays separate from paid IT services.

School support, donated equipment, beneficiary selection, and impact records are handled separately. This page does not accept monetary donations or direct visitors to the business payment number.

How we contribute

Three ways to turn access into confidence.

We focus on support that a school can use, maintain, and build on.

01

Device access

Refurbish and place devices

We assess suitable donated computers, prepare them for safe use, and place them with selected schools when they can make a real difference.

02

Practical learning

Teach practical computer skills

Hands-on sessions can cover computer basics, Office tools, online safety, digital careers, and responsible use of technology.

03

Lasting support

Support school computer labs

We help schools identify basic setup, maintenance, and teacher-support needs so equipment is more likely to remain useful.
