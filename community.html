Accountable Workflow

How Our One-School Support Model Works

A clear process for assessing needs, coordinating equipment and recording delivery.

1

School applies

A school submits a request detailing its learners, location, current equipment, and secure room readiness.

2

Verification & Queue

MosesTechFix inspects room security and power stability, and places the verified school in the upcoming queue.

3

Active Listing

One school is featured as the active drive on our website and channel so community support is 100% focused.

4

Supporters equip school

Donors buy items directly via approved suppliers or offer inspected refurbished computers until target is met.

5

Marked Complete

Equipment is installed and verified. The school is marked COMPLETE, and the next queued school opens!

Transparency & Standards

Trust and Programme Answers

Clear guidelines to protect schools, donors, and learners.

School needs are verified

We confirm that the school exists, that an authorized person requested the help, that a secure room is available, and that learners will directly benefit before publishing a listing.

Refurbished equipment standards

We accept workable laptops, desktops (Core i3 or higher), monitors, mice, keyboards, and power surge protectors. Every unit is inspected and wiped before lab placement.

No cash donations

Community activity stays separate from paid business services. This page does not accept money. Donors buying new equipment coordinate directly with equipment suppliers.

Private Application

Apply for computer support for your school.

This short application helps MosesTechFix Community assess your school’s situation. Submitting opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message; no information is published publicly without review and consent.

  • Primary, secondary, vocational, and community learning centres eligible
  • Requests reviewed privately by MosesTechFix before listing
  • Student names and confidential school records are never published

Please do not include learner names, confidential records, passwords, or payment information.
