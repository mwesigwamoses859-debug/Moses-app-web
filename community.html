MosesTechFix Community

Practical computer access for stronger school communities.

We help schools build digital confidence through suitable computer donations, hands-on learning, and simple support for computer labs.

Apply as a school Donate suitable equipment

A social-impact programme

Technology should open doors for every learner.

MosesTechFix Community is a social-impact programme of MosesTechFix Solutions. It is not a registered NGO. The programme is beginning with practical, school-focused support in Uganda.

Community activity stays separate from paid services.

School support, donated equipment, beneficiary selection, and activity records are handled separately. This page does not accept monetary donations or direct visitors to the business payment number.

How we contribute

Three practical ways to build digital access.

Refurbish and place devices

We assess suitable donated computers, prepare them for safe use, and place them with selected schools when they can make a real difference.

Teach practical computer skills

Hands-on sessions can cover computer basics, Office tools, online safety, digital careers, and responsible use of technology.

Support school computer labs

We help schools identify basic setup, maintenance, and teacher-support needs so equipment is more likely to remain useful.

A clear process

Start small, choose fairly, report honestly.

1

School request

A school tells us about its learners, location, current equipment, and priority need.

2

Needs check

We discuss whether devices, training, or lab support are practical for the school’s setting.

3

Agree support

We confirm what can be provided, the school’s point person, and realistic next steps.

4

Share impact

After activity is complete, we publish only truthful updates and photos with the school’s permission.

School application

Tell us what your school needs.

This short application helps MosesTechFix Community understand where support could be useful. Submitting opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message; no information is stored on this website.

  • Suitable for primary, secondary, vocational, and community-learning settings
  • Requests can be for devices, skills training, lab support, or a combination
  • Selection depends on readiness, need, available resources, and location

Please do not include learner names, confidential records, passwords, or payment information.

Equipment donations

Have a suitable computer to give?

We welcome conversations about workable laptops, desktops, monitors, keyboards, mice, and basic networking equipment. Each item is assessed before it is accepted or matched with a school.
