Build a professional CV in three steps

  1. Add your details. Enter your name, contact information and the role you are applying for. Add your education, work experience and relevant skills.
  2. Review the live preview. Check your spelling, dates and contact details as you go. You can load the fictional example in CVStart to explore the builder before writing your own CV.
  3. Download your PDF. Save your basic CV, check the finished document, and follow the employer's instructions when applying.

For graduates, students and experienced jobseekers

Writing your first CV can feel difficult when you have little work experience. Start with your education, internships, volunteering, practical projects and skills that relate to the vacancy. Use clear examples of what you contributed.

If you already have experience, focus on the work most relevant to your next role. Show responsibilities and real achievements in clear, concise language. CVStart helps organise the document while you provide the facts.

Prepare these details before you start

  • An active phone number or email address and your current town.
  • Your education, qualifications and accurate study dates.
  • Work experience, internships, volunteering or projects, with dates and responsibilities.
  • Relevant skills, languages and genuine achievements.
  • Referee details, if requested, after getting each person's permission.

Make your CV useful for the job you want

Read the vacancy carefully and highlight experience that matches its requirements. Be specific: explain the task, your contribution and any result you can support. Keep the wording easy to scan, and remove details that do not help explain your suitability.

Before sending, check the PDF on your phone or computer and confirm that your contact details work. Include a photo or extra documents when the application instructions ask for them. A clear CV supports an application; interviews and hiring decisions remain with the employer.

Questions about CVStart

Is the CV builder free?

The basic CV builder and PDF download are free. Optional Pro layouts and features are offered inside CVStart. Check the upgrade information there before choosing a paid option.

Can I create a CV on my phone?

Yes. CVStart has a guided browser interface with a live document preview. Open the builder on your phone or computer, enter your details and review your CV before downloading.

Can I use it for my first job or an internship?

Yes. Use your education, projects, volunteering and practical skills to explain what you can contribute. Replace all fictional example details before using the CV for an application.

Does CVStart apply for jobs for me?

No. It helps you prepare a CV. You choose vacancies, check the application instructions and send your own applications.
