Your privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains how MosesTechFix Solutions handles information you share when asking about our services.

Information you share

When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, email, or a website form, you may share your name, contact details, location, device information, and service request details.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, prepare service guidance or a quote, arrange training or support, and provide the service you request.

Website forms and third parties

The service enquiry form uses Google Forms. When you submit it, your name, preferred contact details, service choice and requirements are stored in Google Forms and a private Google Sheet managed by MosesTechFix. Moses receives an email notification. We use the information to respond to your enquiry and manage the agreed work. Other forms may prepare a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Google, WhatsApp, email providers and Cloudflare process information under their own privacy policies.

Website usage

We use Google Analytics to understand page views, YouTube link clicks, video-player loads, contact-button clicks and prepared WhatsApp enquiries. These interactions do not confirm that a message was sent or a booking was made. The contents of request forms are not sent as analytics events. Opening an enquiry form or clicking WhatsApp does not confirm a submission or booking. Actual service enquiries are recorded separately in Google Forms and the private response sheet. Our analytics code respects browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control preferences.

YouTube videos

Featured videos load a YouTube player only when you select “Load video”. The player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube may process information when you load a player or visit its website.

Questions or updates

If you want to ask about information connected to your enquiry, contact MosesTechFix at mosestechfixsolutions@gmail.com or +256 708 262 179.
